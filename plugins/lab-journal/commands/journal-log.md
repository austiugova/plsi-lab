---
name: journal-log
description: Log an experiment in the lab journal from what the researcher gives (text, an export, a photo)
---

Use the `lab-journal` skill to log the experiment the researcher describes after the command, or in the files
they name. Pick or create the entry (`lj list`, `lj template --kind <kind>`), fill only what was given, ask
one question for what is missing and needed (for qPCR: the control group and reference genes), save it, read its
checks as flags, and rebuild the page.
